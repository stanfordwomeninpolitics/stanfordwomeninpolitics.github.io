Education

Education gives the SWIP and greater Stanford community nuanced insight into political issues, ideas, and current events. The team combines policy research, political education, and creative programming to make complex issues engaging and accessible to students. As part of Education, you’ll have the opportunity to research issues you care about, develop educational programming, collaborate with community partners, and gain experience presenting and leading meaningful conversations.

Planned Events and Projects:

  • Healthy Homework Act Implementation: Continue SWIP’s work on California’s Healthy Homework Act (AB 2999) by supporting its implementation and helping develop a youth council in partnership with the California Department of Education.
  • Women in Politics Stanford Course: Work toward developing a Stanford Political Science course on the history of women in American politics, including researching potential topics, figures, movements, and course materials.
  • Teach-Ins on Current Political Issues: Organize interactive teach-ins throughout the year on timely political and policy issues. Past programming has included teach-ins on voting rights and missing and murdered Indigenous women in partnership with SAIO.
  • Politics Through Art, Books, and Culture: Create more informal and creative opportunities to engage with politics, building on past events such as SWIP’s “Prose Before Bros” book club and Art and Activism programming.
  • Policy Speaker and Discussion Events: Bring together students, practitioners, and experts for conversations about pressing policy challenges, building on events such as SWIP’s past discussion with guest speakers on food insecurity and sustainability.
