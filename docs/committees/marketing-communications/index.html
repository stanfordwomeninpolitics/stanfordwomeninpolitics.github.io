Marketing/Communications

Marketing and Communications shapes how SWIP presents itself to the Stanford community and beyond. From managing social media and developing creative campaigns to designing merchandise and promoting SWIP’s events, the Marketing team builds the organization’s public presence and helps members stay connected with everything happening across SWIP. As part of Marketing, you’ll collaborate across committees, experiment with new forms of content, and gain hands-on experience in design, communications, branding, and social media. No prior experience is required: just creativity and a willingness to collaborate!

Current initiatives include:

  • Social Media and Recurring Content: Create engaging and informative content for SWIP’s social media, including World News Wednesdays, SWIP Member Mondays, and quarterly Academic Gems, while developing new series and creative ways to highlight SWIP members, events, and opportunities.
  • SWIP Substack and Newsletter: Work with the Communications team to grow SWIP’s newsletter, sharing organization updates, upcoming opportunities, member highlights, political and policy content, and other resources with the SWIP and broader Stanford communities.
  • Merchandise and SWIP Branding: Design and produce SWIP merchandise, including shirts, stickers, and other creative items, while helping develop a cohesive and recognizable visual identity for the organization.
  • Event Promotion and SWIP-Wide Communications: Collaborate with Programming, Education, Public Service, Fellowships, ComDev, and other teams to create flyers, posters, social media campaigns, and promotional materials for major SWIP events and initiatives throughout the year.
