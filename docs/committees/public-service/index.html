Public Service

Politics is a powerful vehicle for public service: one that can drive reform while strengthening the communities we seek to serve. The Public Service committee connects SWIP’s commitment to political engagement with meaningful, hands-on service in the Bay Area. As SWIP’s newest committee, Public Service gives members the opportunity to move beyond conversations about policy and engage directly with the communities and organizations working on these issues every day. As part of Public Service, you’ll gain hands-on experience in community engagement, nonprofit partnerships, event coordination, advocacy, and service while contributing to meaningful work in our local community.

Planned Events and Projects:

  • Year-Long Nonprofit Partnership: Partner with a Bay Area organization working at the intersection of policy, access, and rights. Members will contribute approximately one hour of service each week to projects shaped by the organization’s needs and priorities.
  • Quarterly SWIP Service Days: Organize a SWIP-wide service opportunity each quarter, bringing together Core and General members to volunteer with nonprofits and community organizations across the Bay Area.
  • Public Service Day: Organize a larger annual service-focused event connecting SWIP members with opportunities to volunteer, learn about local organizations, and explore longer-term pathways into public and community service.
  • Service, Jobs, and Community Opportunities: Help connect SWIP members with additional volunteer, service, and public-interest opportunities throughout the year, making it easier for members to translate their interest in politics and policy into tangible community involvement.
