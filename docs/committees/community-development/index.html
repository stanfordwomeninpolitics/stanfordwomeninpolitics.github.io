Community Development

Community is at the core of everything SWIP does. The Community Development (ComDev) committee creates the spaces that allow SWIP members to build genuine friendships and a strong community outside of meetings and professional events. Throughout the year, ComDev organizes everything from retreats and mentorship opportunities to casual social events and SWIP traditions. As part of ComDev, you’ll help shape SWIP’s culture, welcome new members into the organization, and plan the experiences that make SWIP a close-knit and supportive community.

Planned Events and Projects:

  • Fall and Spring Retreats: Organize SWIP-wide retreats that give members dedicated time to get off campus, bond with one another, and build community at the beginning and throughout the year.
  • SWIP Big Sibling Program: Coordinate SWIP’s sibling program, pairing new and returning members to create mentorship, friendship, and stronger connections across class years and committees.
  • Special D’s: Continue SWIP’s end-of-quarter dinner tradition, bringing the full organization together to celebrate the quarter and spend time with members across committees.
  • Wine & Whines and Recurring Socials: Plan casual, low-pressure opportunities for members to spend time together throughout the year, including Wine & Whines, picnics, hikes, study breaks, and other team-bonding activities.
  • SWIP Holiday and Themed Events: Organize community-wide celebrations throughout the year, building on traditions like SWIP’s Halloween and Galentine’s Day parties.
