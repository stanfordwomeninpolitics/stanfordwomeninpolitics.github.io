Programming

Programming brings exciting political leaders, policymakers, academics, and changemakers to Stanford for conversations with the SWIP community. The team coordinates SWIP’s signature speaker events from start to finish: from identifying and reaching out to speakers to developing thoughtful questions, managing event logistics, and moderating conversations. As part of Programming, you’ll gain hands-on experience with cold outreach, networking, interviewing, public speaking, and event planning while helping bring leaders and role models you admire directly to campus.

Past Events and Projects:

  • International Women’s Week: Help organize a week in the Spring of programming celebrating women in politics, entrepreneurship, law, and other fields, featuring speakers and events that bring together the SWIP and broader Stanford communities.
  • Signature Fireside Chats: Organize intimate conversations with women in politics and public service, building on past events with Congresswomen Zoe Lofgren and Anna Eshoo, former Oakland Mayor Libby Schaaf, and other political leaders.
  • Issue-Focused Panels: Bring together policymakers, practitioners, and experts for deeper conversations around pressing political and policy issues, building on past programming such as SWIP’s Criminal Justice Panel and three-day Native Women in Politics series.
  • Faculty and Expert Conversations: Host conversations with Stanford professors and other experts about their research, careers, and perspectives on major political and policy issues.
  • Public Speaking and Moderation Workshops: Give SWIP members opportunities to strengthen their interviewing, moderating, and public-speaking skills and prepare to facilitate speaker events themselves.
