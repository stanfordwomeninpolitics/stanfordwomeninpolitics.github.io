Professional Development

The Professional Development committee helps SWIP members build the skills, relationships, and resources they need to navigate their academic and professional lives. Through faculty mentorship, career workshops, coffee chats, and resource-sharing, the committee connects members with professors, older students, alumni, and professionals who can provide guidance throughout their time at Stanford and beyond. As part of Professional Development, you’ll help strengthen SWIP’s mentorship network, organize hands-on career programming, and make professional knowledge and opportunities more accessible to members at every stage.

Planned Events and Projects:

  • Faculty Mentorship Program: Expand SWIP’s mentorship program connecting members with Stanford professors for long-term guidance, academic mentorship, and support, building on previous cohorts that matched students with more than 15 faculty members.
  • Resume, Cover Letter, and LinkedIn Workshops: Organize hands-on workshops where members can strengthen their application materials, learn from older SWIP members and professionals, and prepare for internships, fellowships, and post-graduation opportunities.
  • Professor and Professional Coffee Chats: Create informal opportunities for small groups of SWIP members to connect with professors, alumni, and professionals, ask questions about their careers, and build lasting relationships.
  • Career and Campus Resource Guides: Develop accessible guides that help members navigate Stanford and beyond, highlighting professional development resources, academic opportunities, career pathways, and other tools that can often be difficult to find. Past Events and Projects:
  • Faculty mentorship program matching students with 15+ professors for long term guidance and support!
  • Resume and cover letter workshops where older members help younger members strengthen their applications through hands-on editing.
  • Curated resource guides
  • Coffee chats with professors!
