Fellowships

Fellowships expands access to meaningful internships and professional opportunities in public service, policy, advocacy, and related fields. The team develops partnerships with organizations across the country to create internship opportunities specifically for SWIP members while also helping students navigate the broader internship and job-search process. As part of Fellowships, you’ll build relationships with potential partner organizations, help shape SWIP’s summer fellowship program, and organize professional development resources that make competitive opportunities more accessible to our members.

Planned Events and Projects:

  • Expanding SWIP’s Summer Fellowship Program: Build relationships with nonprofits, government offices, advocacy organizations, and other public-service employers across the country to expand the number and range of internship opportunities available to SWIP members.
  • Internship and Fellowship Resources: Compile and share competitive internships, fellowships, and other professional opportunities beyond SWIP’s own placements so members have greater access to opportunities across politics, policy, government, and public service.
  • Professional Development Programming: Organize workshops and events that prepare members for internship and fellowship applications, including programming around resumes, cover letters, interviewing, networking, and navigating the recruiting process.
  • Fellowship Community and Best Practices: Strengthen the experience of SWIP fellows by developing resources and best practices for students participating in internships and maintaining relationships with past and current fellowship partners. Past internship organizations:
  • Immigration Institute of the Bay Area
  • Represent Women
  • Formally
  • Public Citizen
  • End Rape on Campus
  • Accountability Counsel
  • Californians United for a Responsible Budget
  • US Department of Justice
