WHO WE ARE

A community built around policy, law, and public service.

We are SWIP, a community of women who care about the most pressing issues shaping the world around us, and want to do something about it. Across majors, backgrounds, and ambitions, we come together to serve.

OUR STORY

Our story

SWIP began on election night in 2016, when a small group of Stanford women gathered for a presidential election watch party.

In the ten years since, SWIP has grown into a chorus of young women who are passionate about politics, policy and giving back to their communities. Coming from diverse academic backgrounds, our members view SWIP as a space to combine their interests, talents, and ambitions to work on the issues that matter most to them.

As we approach our tenth anniversary, we’d like to think our founding members would be proud to see just how far we’ve come!
